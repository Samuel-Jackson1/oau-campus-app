Field Collector

Stand at the place. Fill the boxes. Tap Save here. The phone records the position for you. You never type coordinates.

You opened this inside another app. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose Open in Chrome or Open in Safari, so location and sending work.

Place = a destination. Landmark = something you pass (kiosk, canteen, Coke House). Bend = a corner in the path (no name needed).
